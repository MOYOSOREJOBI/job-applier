What You’ll Do Position Title: SAP iXp Intern - Full-stack Software Developer Location: Vancouver, BC Expected Start Date: May 2026 Contract Duration: 8 months Work Hours: 40 hours per week The intern will work within the development team of GTLC team. Prior legal compliance knowledge is not required for this position. As a member of the GTLC, you are a self-starter and will work in a dynamic environment to maintain our automation solutions that have been serving as the critical cornerstones to the SAP legal compliances solutions. Test case development for existing and new features. Coordinate testing, integration and documentation of solutions. Provide operational support and new technologies investigations.
