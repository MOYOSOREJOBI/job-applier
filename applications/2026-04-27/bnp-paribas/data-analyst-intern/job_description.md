Note that the position may be in the Canadian Branch of BNP Paribas or in one of its subsidiaries based in Montreal. Internship program As an intern, you will benefit from a 4 month program that aims to give you the tools to develop yourself and discover your potential through customized trainings, networking sessions and transversal projects. More information about the program available on our website: BNP Paribas in Canada Students & Young graduates The position at a glance As an intern, you will assist the Head of Territory (HoT) and the COO with data collection, ad-hoc analyses, and special tasks, preparing presentations and documents for meetings, and supporting discussions with Operations to enhance reports related to Canadian client business. In detail This role will allow you to actively participate in developing data analysis and business reporting capabilities within the Canada CEO & COO Office. You will gain cross-functional knowledge of the various businesses within the Group and develop valuable skills: Data Analysis: You will use and enhance your data analysis skills with tools like Excel, PowerBI, Tableau, and ThinkCell. Project Management: You will learn to manage projects and priorities, monitor progress, report issues, develop action plans. Communication Skills: You will develop strong communication skills by presenting your findings to senior leadership and preparing detailed business reports. Professional Network: You will collaborate with multiple teams across the Bank, opening doors to future opportunities. Career Development: You will collaborate with multiple teams within the Bank, opening potentially leading to various career paths Work conditions: This position provides for standard working conditions in an office and a normal work schedule from Monday to Friday. The Strengths And Skills That Will Help You Succeed Student in a bachelor’s degree in business administration, communication, or other similar field of study Knowledge of Powerpoint Capacity to work with large dataset Knowledge of English and French, written and spoken* Given the vast majority of our clients, both internal and external, are based outside of Quebec and Canada, specific language requirements may apply. Professional working proficiency in French and English languages are required. What’s In It For You In addition to competitive compensation, hybrid work arrangements are available for most positions. In-office presence is required a minimum of 3 days per week, one of which must be on a Monday and/or a Friday. BNP Paribas provides excellent training and personal development programs, as well as opportunities for career development within the company and internationally.
