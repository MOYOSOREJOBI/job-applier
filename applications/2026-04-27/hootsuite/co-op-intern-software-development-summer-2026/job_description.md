&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Hootsuite’s Developers would love to help Computer Science students/ interns develop their technical skills and knowledge about people, process, and technology by working with students over the summer term. You’ll pair with a mentor and work at our Vancouver and/or Toronto office side-by-side with a passionate, egoless team having fun building something bigger than themselves. Experience what it’s like to make a difference in people&#39;s lives by building the products that our customers turn messages into meaningful relationships.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;br&gt;WHAT YOU’LL DO:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with mentors to learn the craft of Software Development&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Collaborate in small teams to design, build, and ship high-quality software to Production&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Participate in code reviews to continually improve your craft and approach to problem solving&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Iterate in an agile fashion; creating hypotheses and testing them with incremental changes&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work-out-loud by sharing your work and experiences internally and with our community&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;You’ll be working with our tech stack (Scala, Javascript, Go and PHP) and with relational databases and non-relational databases (MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, etc.)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;br&gt;WHAT YOU’LL NEED:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience shipping code or a demonstrated interest in programming or technical skills&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A demonstrated history of creative thinking and collaborative problem solving&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A drive to complete things because you want to, not because you have to&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Collaboration and Teamwork: works with others to deliver results, meaningfully contributing to the team and prioritizing group needs over individual needs&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Creativity and Innovation: seeks new and better ways of doing things, generates original and imaginative ideas, products, or solutions&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Courage: steps up to address difficult issues, saying what needs to be said&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Open Communication: clearly conveys thoughts, both written and verbally, listening attentively and asking questions for clarification and understanding&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;WHO YOU ARE:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Tenacious. You are determined to succeed, and you are motivated by the success of customers, colleagues and the community.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Curious. You are always learning and seeking ways to make things better.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Conscientious. You keep your promises, taking your commitments to others seriously, and you have strong integrity.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Humble. You lead with humility and empathy, respecting and learning from the perspectives of others.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
