&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About the Role&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Data is the lifeblood of Klaviyo. As a Data Engineer on this team, you will sit at the intersection of infrastructure and intelligence. You won’t just be moving data; you’ll be building the foundations that power our next generation of AI-driven features and enterprise-scale analytics.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You will bridge the gap between data engineering and product impact. Your work will involve developing scalable Spark pipelines, tuning our storage and query patterns to ensure low-latency performance for our enterprise customers, and modeling the high-impact datasets that drive Klaviyo&#39;s analytics and machine learning engines.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You’ll Do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Build Production-Grade Foundations&lt;/strong&gt;: Develop and maintain scalable data pipelines and core tables using PySpark, Airflow, and dbt. You will implement the foundational datasets that power our AI, ML, and Analytics products.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Optimize for Enterprise Performance&lt;/strong&gt;: Tune Spark jobs and storage patterns to ensure low-latency data retrieval. You will help implement materialized views and efficient partitioning strategies to support high-performance reporting at scale.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Treat Data as a Product&lt;/strong&gt;: Contribute to the full lifecycle of datasets. This includes defining clear data contracts with upstream teams, writing maintainable code via peer reviews, and ensuring every asset is well-documented and trusted by downstream users.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Drive Operational Excellence&lt;/strong&gt;: Ensure the reliability of our data engine by monitoring for freshness, volume anomalies, and schema changes. You will be responsible for ensuring that when a customer loads a dashboard, the data is accurate and on time.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Partner Cross-Functionally&lt;/strong&gt;: Collaborate with Product, Engineering, and AI/ML teams to define consistent metrics that align with business goals. You will act as a bridge to ensure new features land with robust data support.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Innovate with AI&lt;/strong&gt;: Look for opportunities to put AI at the center of your workflow, whether it is using AI to generate tests, detect data anomalies, or accelerate complex analysis.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Who You Are&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;The Experience:&lt;/strong&gt; 2+ years of experience in data engineering or a data-intensive software engineering role. You’ve moved past the &quot;beginner&quot; phase and are comfortable taking a project from a design doc to a production deployment.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Fluent in SQL and Python:&lt;/strong&gt; You have a solid grasp of SQL for high-performance querying and are comfortable using Python for data manipulation and automation. You focus on writing code that balances speed with reliability.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Distributed Systems Knowledge:&lt;/strong&gt; You have hands-on experience with Spark (PySpark/SparkSQL) and understand how to tune jobs for performance in a cloud environment (AWS/EMR).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Modeling Intuition:&lt;/strong&gt; You understand the difference between a &quot;raw table&quot; and a &quot;semantic layer.&quot; You’ve worked with modern modeling tools (like dbt) and understand partitioning, schema evolution, and lakehouse concepts (Iceberg/Delta).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Performance Minded:&lt;/strong&gt; You care about latency. You enjoy the challenge of making a query run faster and understand how to use materialized views and caching effectively.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Collaborative &amp;amp; Curious:&lt;/strong&gt; You’re an inclusive collaborator who enjoys working with Product and Data Science. You’re excited to experiment with AI tools to make your own engineering workflow more efficient.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Nice to Haves&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience with Iceberg table maintenance and compaction.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Exposure to Terraform or other Infrastructure-as-Code tools.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A background in Martech or SaaS platforms dealing with high-frequency event data.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience building data products that directly power customer-facing UI components and/or support AI/ML features.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience building near real-time or streaming pipelines for user-facing analytics or monitoring.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hands-on work with analytics engineering tools and practices (e.g., dbt, metrics layers, semantic models).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Familiarity with statistical modeling and machine learning.&lt;/li&gt;
