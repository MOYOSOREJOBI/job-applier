&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with product and engineering managers to implement business-critical features enabling new user experiences across the entire stack (React Native for FE and Python, Kotlin for BE)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with team technical leaders to design and implement insightful, forward-looking approaches that go beyond the direct team and solve significant open-ended problems.&lt;/li&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Collaborate with the design team to ensure a visually appealing and user-friendly interface.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Build relationships with people across engineering and other organizations for project design, implementation, roll-out, and maintenance.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Implement robust analytics and tracking to measure the effectiveness of acquisition efforts.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with other backend engineers to integrate with APIs and data sources.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Participate in code reviews and ensure code quality and adherence to best practices.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Stay up-to-date on the latest web development technologies and trends.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;

&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What We Look For&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;2+ years of experience as a full stack developer&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Prior experience building and maintaining complex full stack systems with cross-browser support&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Familiarity/Exposure to: React Native, Python / Java / Kotlin&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Knowledge of modern front-end tech stack (we use Javascript, Typescript, React/Redux, ES6, Webpack, and CSS3)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Prior experience with monitoring and alerting systems (Sentry.io, Rollbar, Chronosphere, Kibana)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience with A/B testing and experimentation&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A pragmatic, user-focused approach to delivering high-quality features safely and incrementally&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A self-driven and motivated engineer who takes pride and ownership in navigating through ambiguities and solving complex technical/organizational problems&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Passion for working with cross-functional teams, including product, design, analytics, and business teams&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Eager to learn new things and have a growth mindset&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
