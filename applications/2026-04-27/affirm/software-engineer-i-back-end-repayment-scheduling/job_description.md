&lt;div class=&quot;content-intro&quot;&gt;&lt;p&gt;Affirm is reinventing credit to make it more honest and friendly, giving consumers the flexibility to buy now and pay later without any hidden fees or compounding interest.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Repayment Scheduling engineering team is growing rapidly, and we&#39;re looking for a Software Engineer specializing in Backend development. We&#39;re a fully European engineering team – distributed across Poland and Spain with overlapping working hours. We build and own the system that enables our users to schedule automatic payments to pay back loans worth collectively over $30B each year. We directly improve the financial lives of over 20 million people, giving them peace of mind that their payments will be made on time.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The team was created recently with brand new scope, and you would be one of the first engineers on this team. This means an outsized opportunity to influence how a core part of Affirm&#39;s infrastructure gets built. This role rewards curiosity, ownership, and a builder&#39;s mindset. You&#39;ll know exactly what your objectives are, you&#39;ll have what you need to hit them, and you&#39;ll choose your own path to get there. You&#39;ll learn quickly from experienced teammates and grow your craft on problems that matter at scale.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;If you want to build something new and operate it at scale – come join us!&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You&#39;ll Do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;br&gt;· With the support of your team, you will work on tasks that contribute to the team&#39;s projects and goals.&lt;br&gt;· You will work collaboratively and proactively with your team and stakeholders, bringing them along for your work and helping to create visibility and dialog regarding the risks and trade-offs related to your work.&lt;br&gt;· You will strike the right balance of speed and quality in your work, ensuring that we hit our business goals while protecting our systems from downtime.&lt;br&gt;· You will contribute to a sense of community on your team by engaging in growth and development activities.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What We Look For&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;br&gt;· You have previous work or internship experience designing, developing and launching backend systems at scale and are experienced using one of Python or Kotlin.&lt;br&gt;· You are familiar with the building blocks of distributed systems, and the technologies like AWS, MySQL and Kubernetes.&lt;br&gt;· You have mastered taking a simple problem or business scenario into a solution that interacts with multiple software components, and executing on it by writing clear, easily understood, well tested and extensible code.&lt;br&gt;· You are comfortable navigating a large code base, debugging others&#39; code, and providing feedback to other engineers through code reviews.&lt;br&gt;· Your experience demonstrates that you take ownership of your growth, proactively seeking feedback from your team, your manager, and your stakeholders.&lt;br&gt;· You have strong verbal and written communication skills that support effective collaboration with our global engineering team.&lt;/p&gt;
