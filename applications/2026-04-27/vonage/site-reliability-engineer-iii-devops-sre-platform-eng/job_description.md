&lt;div class=&quot;content-intro&quot;&gt;&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Join Vonage and help us innovate cloud communications for businesses worldwide!&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Why this role matters:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a Platform Engineer, you are a force multiplier for our engineering teams. You don’t just manage servers; you build and maintain the cloud-native foundations that make shipping code at Vonage fast, safe, and easy. Your mission is to &lt;strong&gt;manage robust infrastructure while eliminating developer friction through simple-to-use automation&lt;/strong&gt;, ensuring our production APIs remain highly available, scalable, and cost-efficient.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Your key responsibilities:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Infrastructure Management:&lt;/strong&gt; Take ownership of our cloud-native footprint. You will lead the design, provisioning, and maintenance of resilient infrastructure, ensuring our environments are stable and meet high-availability requirements.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Build Self-Service Tools:&lt;/strong&gt; Help build and evolve our internal developer platform. You’ll use &lt;strong&gt;Go&lt;/strong&gt; (predominantly) to create workflows that automate repetitive tasks—like infrastructure provisioning, restarting deployments, or managing service health.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Kubernetes Operations:&lt;/strong&gt; Act as a key technical resource for our large-scale Kubernetes clusters. You’ll dive deep into the stack to troubleshoot complex networking or performance bottlenecks that others can&#39;t solve.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Automate Guardrails:&lt;/strong&gt; Use &lt;strong&gt;Kyverno&lt;/strong&gt; to bake security and best practices directly into the cluster. You’ll ensure that as we scale, our clusters stay compliant and healthy without slowing down development.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Streamline Delivery:&lt;/strong&gt; Own the &quot;path to production&quot; by optimizing our &lt;strong&gt;GitHub Actions&lt;/strong&gt; and &lt;strong&gt;ArgoCD&lt;/strong&gt; pipelines, ensuring global teams can deploy code safely through GitOps.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h2 class=&quot;no-bottom-margin&quot;&gt;What you&#39;ll bring&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Experience:&lt;/strong&gt; 5+ years in DevOps, Platform Engineering, or SRE roles with a heavy focus on cloud-native architecture.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Kubernetes Knowledge:&lt;/strong&gt; Practical experience managing and troubleshooting production &lt;strong&gt;EKS or GKE&lt;/strong&gt; clusters. You should be comfortable &quot;under the hood&quot; of a cluster.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Programming Skills:&lt;/strong&gt; Strong proficiency in &lt;strong&gt;Go&lt;/strong&gt; (preferred) or &lt;strong&gt;Python&lt;/strong&gt;. We are looking for someone who can build tools, APIs, or custom controllers to automate complex logic.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;CI/CD &amp;amp; GitOps:&lt;/strong&gt; Hands-on experience with &lt;strong&gt;ArgoCD&lt;/strong&gt; and &lt;strong&gt;GitHub Actions&lt;/strong&gt; (specifically creating reusable workflows and custom actions).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Policy &amp;amp; Config:&lt;/strong&gt; Experience using &lt;strong&gt;Helm&lt;/strong&gt; for packaging and &lt;strong&gt;Kyverno&lt;/strong&gt; (or OPA) for policy enforcement.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Infrastructure as Code:&lt;/strong&gt; Mastery of &lt;strong&gt;Terraform&lt;/strong&gt;, with an interest in moving toward Kubernetes-native management (like &lt;strong&gt;Crossplane&lt;/strong&gt;).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;System Reliability:&lt;/strong&gt; A deep commitment to infrastructure stability and the ability to manage complex cloud environments at scale.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;The Troubleshooting Mindset:&lt;/strong&gt; You enjoy solving &quot;hard&quot; problems and can navigate complex distributed systems to find root causes.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Observability:&lt;/strong&gt; Experience using tools like &lt;strong&gt;Prometheus&lt;/strong&gt; or &lt;strong&gt;Grafana&lt;/strong&gt; to monitor system health and performance.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Efficiency:&lt;/strong&gt; A &quot;FinOps&quot; mindset - you care about right-sizing resources so we aren&#39;t over-provisioning or wasting budget.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
